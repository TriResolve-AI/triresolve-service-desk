## 📌 What is TriResolve AI?

TriResolve AI is an Intelligent Service Desk system powered by **TriNexa**, our global AI assistant.
It automates organizational workflows across IT, HR, and Finance through reasoning-based agents that collaborate to resolve tickets end-to-end.
